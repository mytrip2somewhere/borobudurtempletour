Three ways to climb Borobudur, compared honestly

Every tour here departs Yogyakarta with hotel pickup, includes the temple climb, and continues to Prambanan. They differ in when the day starts, what else it covers, and how long you are out. Sorted by how many travellers have booked each one.

Short answer

Most travellers book the Sunrise Climb & Prambanan tour: dawn from Punthuk Setumbu hill, then guaranteed climb access at the temple, then Prambanan. Want a volcano in the same day? Take the 12-hour Merapi version. Prefer a later start with no dawn alarm? The 8-hour day tour climbs Borobudur, adds Candi Mendut, and still reaches Prambanan.

One honest note on sunrise, because the tour titles blur it: both dawn tours watch sunrise from Punthuk Setumbu, a hill facing the temple, and climb Borobudur afterward. The separate official product that puts you on the monument itself at 04:00 is explained in our sunrise guide; these tours are not that.

Where the day takes you

Borobudur sits about an hour northwest of Yogyakarta; Prambanan is on the city's eastern edge. Every tour above connects them in one loop with hotel pickup.

Common questions

Do these tours include the Borobudur entry ticket?

The tours bundle transport, a guide, and entry arrangements; exactly what is bundled varies by tour and option, so check the inclusions on the live listing before booking. Our tickets guide explains what the entry itself costs, so you can judge the bundle.

Where do the sunrise tours actually watch sunrise from?

From Punthuk Setumbu, a hill about 2.5 km from the monument with a view across the mist toward Borobudur. The climb happens after. Sunrise from the temple structure itself is a separate official product, covered in the sunrise guide.

Do I need to book ahead?

The listings show live availability by date, so the honest answer is to pick your date and look. Dawn slots are the ones most affected by demand.
