Disclosure

How this site makes money

Affiliate commissions, stated plainly. Here is what that means for you and for what you read here.

The short version

Tour links on this site go to the operators' official listings on GetYourGuide and Viator. If you book through one of those links, the platform pays us a commission. You pay nothing extra; the price is the same as arriving at the listing directly.

What an affiliate link is here

When a page says "check live availability and prices", the button carries our partner ID for GetYourGuide or Viator. The platform tracks that you came from us, and if you complete a booking it pays us a share of its own fee. The tour operator's price to you does not change. That commission is how the site is funded; there are no ads, no sponsored posts, and no paywall.

We only link tours we actually feature

Every affiliate link on this site points to one of the three tours described in full on our tour pages. We do not scatter links to products we have not written about, and we do not swap in whichever tour pays the highest rate. If a tour appears here, it has its own page explaining who it suits and who it does not, and the comparison page is willing to tell you that a given tour is the wrong pick for you.

Where the ratings and prices come from

The ratings and review counts you see here are the operators' own listing figures on GetYourGuide and Viator, quoted as published. We do not generate, adjust, or aggregate them ourselves.

Prices are dynamic. They move with dates, group sizes, seasons, and the platforms' own pricing. That is why our pages avoid printing tour prices as fixed facts: the live listing is authoritative, and the figure you see there for your date is the real one. The same logic drives our tickets guide, which quotes only figures the official operator publishes.

Does the commission shape the advice?

The honest answer: the incentive exists, and the guard against it is checkability. Every factual claim on this site traces to the official operator or the listings themselves, and where a number could not be verified we say so rather than printing it. When the right answer for a reader is "none of these tours", as it is for people chasing sunrise on the monument itself, we say that too.
