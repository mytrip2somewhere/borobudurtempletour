Talk to us

We are a small operation, so there is no ticket system and no chatbot. There is an inbox, and a real person reads it.

For any question, email us at info@borobudurtempletour.org. We reply as fast as we can. Ask about the tours, the tickets, the sunrise options, or anything in the guides that reads unclear.

Email info@borobudurtempletour.org

One thing to know before you write: we are not the temple's operator and we do not sell tickets ourselves. Bookings happen on the operators' official GetYourGuide and Viator listings, so questions about an existing booking go to the platform you booked on. Everything else, we are glad to answer.

If your question is which tour to book, the comparison page may answer it faster than email: it gives a verdict by visitor type across all three tours.
